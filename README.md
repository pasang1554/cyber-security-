# 🔐 90 Days of Cybersecurity

### 🚀 My Journey from Beginner to Cybersecurity Student

Welcome to my **90 Days of Cybersecurity Challenge!** This repository documents my daily learning journey as I build a strong foundation in cybersecurity, computer networking, Linux, ethical hacking, and digital forensics.

My goal is to develop practical cybersecurity skills, complete hands-on labs, document my progress, and prepare for future opportunities in the cybersecurity field.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 📚 Build a strong foundation in cybersecurity.
- 🌐 Understand computer networks and network security.
- 🐧 Learn Linux commands and system administration.
- 🔍 Understand ethical hacking and penetration testing.
- 🛡️ Learn security tools and vulnerability assessment.
- 🧪 Practice through hands-on labs and authorized environments.
- 🔐 Explore cryptography, encryption, and digital forensics.
- 📝 Document daily progress, notes, commands, and practical exercises.
- 💼 Build a cybersecurity portfolio on GitHub.

---

| Phase 9 | Day 81–90 | Projects, Revision, and Portfolio | Complete practical projects and review the skills learned |

*This roadmap is a flexible learning plan. Topics and timelines may change as I progress.*

## 🧪 Practical Learning Approach

My daily learning process will follow these steps:

1. **Learn:** Study the concepts and understand how they work.
2. **Document:** Write notes and record important commands.
3. **Practice:** Perform hands-on exercises in a safe, authorized lab.
4. **Review:** Test my understanding with questions and challenges.
5. **Publish:** Upload my notes and progress to GitHub.

---

## 🔒 Ethical and Responsible Learning

Cybersecurity knowledge comes with responsibility.

- Practice scanning and testing only systems I own or have explicit permission to assess.
- Use intentionally vulnerable applications and authorized training environments.
- Never access, modify, or disclose another person's data without authorization.
- Follow applicable laws, platform rules, and responsible disclosure practices.

My goal is to learn how to identify security weaknesses and help protect systems, networks, and users.
